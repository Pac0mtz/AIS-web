# Asset credits and production review

## AIS brand artwork

The stacked AIS logo and circular AIS badge are user-supplied master artwork. The custom service, industry, and project-type icons were created for this website in the AIS blue/red palette.

## Photography status

All third-party photography is used only as representative imagery in this private preview. It does not document AIS projects or personnel. Source, license, and permission review must be completed before production publication. No photograph should be represented as an AIS project photo without the owner's confirmation.

The original 17 service hero images and six project-type images also remain subject to that production review.
